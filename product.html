The Genesis of Modern Specialty

The Peterson Legacy in Boquete Highlands

In 2004, Daniel Peterson discovered that coffee from the high-altitude parcels of Jaramillo tasted profoundly unlike anything previously cupped in the Western Hemisphere: a burst of bergamot, tea florals, and candied peaches. Thus was born the legendary Esmeralda Special.

Nestled along the slopes of Volcán Barú, the cold Pacific and Caribbean air masses meet directly over the micro-valley, causing perpetual cooling mists known locally as bajareque. This microclimate stalls the cherry maturation process to an astonishing 240 days, concentrating sugars and aromatic esters deep within the seed.

Extraction Stages

01

0:00 — Bloom & Degas (50g)

Gently pour 50g of water with spiral motion. Swirl softly once and let rest for 45 seconds to liberate volatile citrus esters.

02

0:45 — The Center Flow (up to 180g)

Pour in continuous controlled concentric circles up to 180g by 1:15. Maintain calm water surface without turbulence.

03

1:30 — Final Sweetness Extension (up to 300g)

Even pour to 300g by 2:00. Give one single gentle tap to settle the coffee bed. Let draw down complete at approximately 3:15.
